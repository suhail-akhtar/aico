# Code of Conduct

AICO is a small project. We want it to be a place where anyone can report a
bug, ask a question or propose a change without being made to feel small.

**Expected:** be respectful and assume good faith; critique the work, not the
person; be patient with newcomers; say plainly when you were wrong; keep
discussions on topic.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults or personal attacks; discrimination
of any kind; sexualised language or imagery; publishing someone's private
information; sustained disruption; pressuring anyone to share credentials or
private data.

**Scope:** issues, pull requests, discussions, advisories and any space where
you represent the project.

**Reporting:** contact the maintainer privately through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/suhail-akhtar/aico/security/advisories/new)
(it is the one private channel the repository offers), marked "conduct".
Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove
contributions, and warn, suspend or ban participants, in proportion to the
behaviour.
